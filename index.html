Is this the original bottle?

No. You're buying a decanted fragrance in a RIĦA travel atomiser or sample bottle — not the original manufacturer's bottle.

Is it the real thing?

Yes. RIĦA decants authentic luxury and niche fragrances, purchased from reputable suppliers.

How long does a 30ml last?

Roughly 300–400 sprays, depending on atomiser output and spraying technique.

Can I try before I commit?

That's the whole idea. Start with a 2ml sample, then move up to a 30ml when you're sure.

Where do you deliver?

Across Malta, to begin with.

Are you affiliated with the brands?

No. RIĦA is an independent retailer and decanting service, not affiliated with or endorsed by the designer or niche brands whose fragrances it sells.
